You may have noticed that there is a `Constants` file in the `pedroPathing`
package. This is where you configure all your constants for Pedro. It
contains three types of constants:

1. **Foresight (Algorithm) constants** consists of values from the
automatic tuners.
2. **Drivetrain constants** contains constants specific to your drivetrain
type. For example, mecanum drivetrain constants contains the motor names.
3. **Localizer constants** contains constants specific to your localizer. For
example, OTOS constants include the hardware map name of the OTOS and the
offset.

There is also a `create` method that constructs the follower. You
will call this method in your OpModes.

you through modifying it to suit your robot.
