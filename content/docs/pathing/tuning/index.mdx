Pedro Pathing utilizes AutoTune, a robot-hosted webpage, that allows for easy tuning of your robot's drivetrain, localizer, and the Foresight algorithm.

Please ensure you properly [installed](/docs/pathing/installation) Pedro Pathing before continuing.

The following steps will guide you through tuning your robot:

<Steps>
    <Step>
        <h3><Link href="/docs/pathing/tuning/drivetrain">Drivetrain</Link></h3>

        In this step, you set constants for your drivetrain such as your motor names and directions.
    </Step>
    <Step>
        <h3>
            <Link href="/docs/pathing/tuning/localization">Localization</Link>
        </h3>

        In this step, you tune your localizer, which allows for your robot to know its location on the field.
    </Step>
    <Step>
        <h3><Link href="/docs/pathing/tuning/foresight">Foresight</Link></h3>

        In this step, you tune Foresight, which is the built-in algorithm that allows your robot to follow paths accurately.
    </Step>
</Steps>
